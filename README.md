# Ride A Fish

A Decentraland SDK7 test scene by Pandaymeta Studio for Creator Success V0.

Ride a fish around a tropical island. Eat green food and smaller fish to grow, evade the Hammerhead and larger riders, then park at the dock. The Fish Index tracks discoveries, and signed-in players keep their discoveries and coins across sessions.

## Play the sample

Open the project in Decentraland Creator Hub and choose **Play → Desktop Client**. Press **E** near Rean for the tutorial, or beside your fish to board. Use **WASD** to swim and **Space** to jump. Press **E** at the dock to park your fish at its current size.

The test scene is playable locally. Public deployment to **entenium.dcl.eth** is pending.

## Build

Use Node.js 18 or newer.

```sh
npm ci
npm run build
```

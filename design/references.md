# Design references

## 8 November 2026 — GDD writing and V0 scope

- **Owner-supplied reference:** [Claim Bite GDD](https://github.com/carlosmu/dcl-claim-bite/blob/master/design/gdd.md), read 8 November 2026. The owner asked how its presentation could improve Ride A Fish's V0 application.
- **Writing lessons used:** A clear loop, an explicit minimum deliverable, and a direct explanation of how another player's action changes play. Its mining/economy mechanics were not adopted.
- **Limits:** This is a working GDD, not evidence of funding approval, player enjoyment, or a program rule. Its internal draft notes and milestone labels are not requirements for Ride A Fish.
- **Program framing:** [Creator Success onboarding](https://docs.decentraland.org/creator/studio-onboarding), reviewed 8 November 2026. V0 tests an agreed small vertical slice; a four-week V1 scope follows a successful V0. Our GDD keeps its proposed V0 slice separate from its provisional V1 outlook.
